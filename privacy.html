Privacy at a glance

Veyro is designed to announce high-confidence incoming-payment alerts from notifications you choose to share on your Android phone. The core detection and text-to-speech features run on your device. Veyro is not a bank, does not verify settlement, and does not use payment notifications for advertising.

Our principlePayment-notification content is processed locally to provide the announcement feature. It is not sent to Veyro for cloud speech or analytics.

Information we process

On your device

When you grant Android Notification Access, Veyro can read notification metadata and text from payment apps you select. It uses that information to identify likely incoming payments. Local history may store parsed details such as amount, time, source, sender when detected, and reference ID when detected. Your settings, selected language, and merchant profile information are also stored on your device.

For sign-in and service operation

If sign-in is enabled for your build, Veyro sends your phone number and the one-time code you enter to the configured Veyro authentication server to verify your account. That service creates and maintains an authenticated session and device association. Authentication tokens are kept in your device’s secure storage.

Website information

This website does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The theme choice is saved only in your browser’s local storage so your selected appearance can be remembered. If you email us, we receive the information you include in that message.

How information is used

  • To detect and speak matching payment notifications on your device.
  • To show local payment history and summaries in the app.
  • To authenticate your account and maintain a secure session when server sign-in is configured.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell payment-notification content or use it for targeted advertising. We do not use it to make lending, credit, or other automated decisions about you.

Sharing and security

Veyro does not share payment-notification content with payment apps, advertisers, or analytics providers. The configured authentication service necessarily receives the data used for OTP authentication and session management. Data transmitted to that service is sent over the app’s configured secure connection. No system can promise absolute security; keep your phone secured and revoke Notification Access if you no longer want Veyro to process notifications.

Your choices and controls

  • Notification Access: turn it off at any time in Android settings. Veyro will then stop receiving payment notifications.
  • Local history: clear it from Veyro Settings when you no longer want it stored on the device.
  • Account session: sign out in the app to remove local session credentials.
  • Website preference: change the light/dark control at the top of this page, or clear your browser’s site data.

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights relating to personal data. You may contact us to request help with those rights.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Veyro’s features or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version here and change the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@veyro.app.
