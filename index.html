GOOD TO KNOW

A few honest answers.

Does Veyro verify bank settlements?+

No. Veyro announces notifications that match its on-device rules; it is not a bank-verified payment terminal. Always verify high-value payments in the payment or banking app.

What does Veyro need access to?+

It needs Android Notification Access to read payment notifications you choose to share. You can turn this access off at any time in Android settings.

Will it work with a Bluetooth speaker?+

Veyro uses normal Android media playback. If Android routes media to your connected Bluetooth speaker, announcements play there too.
